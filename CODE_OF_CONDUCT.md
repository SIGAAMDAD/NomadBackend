# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

NomadFramework exists to provide a high-performance, engine-agnostic C# framework that can be reused across projects without reinventing core runtime systems. That goal requires a technical community where contributors can discuss architecture, performance, APIs, bugs, and design tradeoffs directly while still treating each other with professionalism.

This Code of Conduct applies to all NomadFramework spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, Discord, documentation, examples, and any other project-controlled communication channel.

## Expected Behavior

Project participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, direct, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, design, benchmarks, documentation, or behavior, not on the person.
- Assume good faith unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
- Provide technical reasoning when disagreeing.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant to NomadFramework and its ecosystem.
- Respect maintainers’ decisions about scope, architecture, licensing, performance goals, and project direction.
- Follow the repository’s formatting, documentation, testing, and licensing requirements when contributing code.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Personal attacks, insults, slurs, or demeaning language.
- Discrimination based on identity, background, ability, nationality, religion, age, gender, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Bad-faith arguments, trolling, brigading, or deliberate derailment.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction.
- Submitting intentionally misleading bug reports, benchmarks, security claims, or licensing claims.
- Using project spaces to promote unrelated products, communities, or disputes.

## Technical Disagreement

NomadFramework is performance-sensitive and architecture-heavy. Strong disagreement is allowed. Hostility is not.

When disagreeing, contributors should:

- Explain the tradeoff.
- Provide examples, benchmarks, references, or implementation details where possible.
- Distinguish preference from correctness.
- Avoid framing another contributor’s work as incompetent or malicious.
- Accept that maintainers may reject technically valid ideas if they do not fit the project’s design goals.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and may take action when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Maintainer actions may include:

- Asking participants to revise wording or tone.
- Editing or removing comments.
- Closing or locking issues or pull requests.
- Removing off-topic or abusive content.
- Temporarily or permanently restricting participation.
- Reporting severe abuse to the relevant platform.

Maintainers should apply this Code of Conduct consistently and in proportion to the behavior involved.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainer through an appropriate private channel when available.

When reporting, include:

- A description of what happened.
- Links, screenshots, or message references if available.
- The people involved.
- Whether the issue is ongoing.
- Any safety or privacy concerns.

Reports will be reviewed with respect for privacy and handled as promptly as practical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and may also apply when a person represents NomadFramework in public spaces, such as by using the project name, repository, official links, or project-maintained community channels.

## Enforcement

Enforcement decisions are made by project maintainers. The goal is to protect the health of the project and its contributors, not to punish honest mistakes.

Participants who are asked to stop unacceptable behavior are expected to comply immediately.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is project-specific and written for NomadFramework.
